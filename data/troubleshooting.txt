CUSTOMER SUPPORT TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Device Not Turning On

If the device does not turn on, first verify that the power cable
is securely connected to both the device and the electrical outlet.

If the device still does not start, disconnect the power cable,
wait 30 seconds, reconnect it, and press the power button.

If the power indicator flashes red three times, the device may
have a power supply issue. Contact technical support.

Device Running Slowly

If the device is running slowly, restart the device.

Check whether a software update is available under
Settings > System > Software Update.

If storage usage is above 90%, remove unnecessary files or
applications.
